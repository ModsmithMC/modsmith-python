# Guides

Each guide covers one task. They can be read in any order.

:::{container} cards

- ![](../assets/pixel/search.svg) [Search and download](platforms.md)

  Find projects on Modrinth and CurseForge, and download releases.

- ![](../assets/pixel/link.svg) [Resolve dependencies](dependencies.md)

  Work out which mods a mod needs, and identify files by their contents.

- ![](../assets/pixel/package.svg) [Read a modpack](modpacks.md)

  Open an archive and see what is inside.

- ![](../assets/pixel/layers.svg) [Install a mod loader](loaders.md)

  Set up Forge, NeoForge, Fabric or Quilt.

- ![](../assets/pixel/refresh.svg) [Install and update instances](instances.md)

  Keep a pack up to date and preserve player changes.

- ![](../assets/pixel/download.svg) [Add mods and packs](additions.md)

  Install, update and remove single mods, resource packs and shaders.

- ![](../assets/pixel/key.svg) [Install CurseForge packs](curseforge.md)

  Look up files and handle manual downloads.

- ![](../assets/pixel/play.svg) [Work with the official launcher](launcher.md)

  Add, list and remove launcher profiles.

- ![](../assets/pixel/cup.svg) [Manage Java](java.md)

  Find or download the runtime a version needs.

- ![](../assets/pixel/clock.svg) [Show progress and cancel](progress.md)

  Connect long-running calls to your interface.

- ![](../assets/pixel/alert.svg) [Handle errors](errors.md)

  Catch failures and tune retries.

- ![](../assets/pixel/puzzle.svg) [Extend Modsmith](extending.md)

  Add your own loaders, pack formats and platforms.

:::
